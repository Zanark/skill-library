# Single-host gesture study

Prototype stepped near-arm movement in a large single-host card.

- **Inputs:** newly permitted art/speech, isolated cuff/hand masks and protected
  background/prop regions.
- **Mechanism:** a fresh isolated study with actual limb isolation and stationary
  background reconstruction.
- **Result:** a scoped portrait motion prototype, not a general rig or permanent default.
- **Review:** encoded before/after poses and protected laptop/cup/microphone pixels.
