# Explicit speech-first music and style edits

This sanitized public reference omits private batch histories and approval
records. The original complete contract remains in the private migration
archive. Follow the canonical skill and CLI help for the exact plan schema.

Music/style finishing is an explicit per-cut vertical option for LeetCode and
System Design, not an ordinary default, automatic downloader or studio control.
Horizontal complete solves are unchanged.

**Implemented:** mixing/editing infrastructure. **Incomplete:** verified
current-trend discovery, full-duration matching and integrated three-mode
selection. A successful mixer demo is not evidence of Instagram relevance.

## Select the requested route

- Recommendation only: preserve original audio, media hashes and review
  signatures; suggest an exact recording with a clip-specific reason.
- Licensed local mix: use only explicitly authorized audio with verified
  recording/version, synchronization/distribution rights, source evidence,
  hashes and complete required attribution.
- Native-platform handoff: require actual account/region/library availability
  evidence for any claim. A search result or popular song is insufficient.

Confirm audience, platform, account/library constraints and desired treatment
from the actual request. Do not import a historical batch exception into new
work. Do not substitute random/generated music for a trend request, claim
virality, or silently download/embed a recommendation.

When adding music, prefer different recordings; a reused recording needs
meaningfully different sections with exact start/end and prior-use comparison.
A tiny offset, gain change or transcoding does not create genuine variety.

## Mixing and review

Inspect `workflow style --help` and `workflow preview --help`. Use speech-first
beat handles, safe short transitions and restrained ducked music where requested.
Keep the narration, source maps, qualifications and original source untouched.
Verify complete output duration and timing rather than only a short preview.

Styling/mixing changes the draft and invalidates affected hash-bound evidence.
Generate fresh independent output ASR, ordinary review and immutable publication
receipts for the new exact media. Never bypass these gates because an earlier
unstyled clip was approved. Preserve old versions and unrelated exports.

## Credits and delivery

Keep required music credits in a separate clean copyable field/file, not mixed
with authored captions, hashtags or technical mix notes. Explain where the
licence requires actual posting attribution; a local credits file alone does
not satisfy a description/caption credit requirement.

Captions stay hashtag-free; provide a separate Hashtags field with at most five
relevant unique lowercase tags and at most 1,000 characters including spaces
and `#`. Use genuine one-click copy buttons. A manual fallback after denial is
useful but is not a successful automatic copy.

Keep one gallery for the original request, exact recording identity, rights
evidence, source/output hashes and review limitations. Do not upload, imply
platform acceptance, or claim user approval from successful encoding.
