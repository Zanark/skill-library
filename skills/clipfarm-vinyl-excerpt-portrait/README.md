# Portrait vinyl music excerpt

Recompose an explicitly selected song passage into a portrait platter/sleeve film.

- **Inputs:** a permitted selected passage and canonical art, with exact range/
  audio provenance.
- **Mechanism:** the new-production or qualified range/recomposition path;
  not an automatic crop of a landscape movie.
- **Result:** a subtitle-free portrait excerpt and center-safe matching thumbnail.
- **Review:** selected range, authorized fades, aspect, art/lettering, shared
  background and actual full/center cover.

Read [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) and [RUNBOOK.md](RUNBOOK.md). A current ClipFarm
checkout and permitted assets are required; do not add unrelated music or subtitles.
