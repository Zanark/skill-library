# Animated teaching with moving camera

Combine causal teaching with a genuine synchronized source-camera inset.

- **Inputs:** reviewed speech, the complete camera crop and one source-time map.
- **Mechanism:** a fresh local compositor decoding retained camera footage
  against the same source clock as speech and actions.
- **Result:** a 9:16 animation with a padded top-right moving inset.
- **Review:** actual movement, joins, full crop and separation from teaching,
  headings and captions.

Read [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) and [RUNBOOK.md](RUNBOOK.md). Requires a current
ClipFarm checkout. Never replace moving source footage with a still or unrelated loop.
