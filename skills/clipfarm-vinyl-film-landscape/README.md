# Full-song landscape picture-disc film

Show a complete original song with a rotating printed disc, separate sleeve and fixed lighting.

- **Inputs:** permitted song, canonical circular/square artwork and exact audio/
  rights metadata.
- **Mechanism:** a new local compositor sharing the saved art-derived background
  and object-local groove/reflection treatment.
- **Result:** a full-song landscape music film and matching cover.
- **Review:** full audio/handles, encoded motion, lettering, light behavior and cover.

Read [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) and [RUNBOOK.md](RUNBOOK.md). Use a current ClipFarm
checkout. Do not claim physical simulation or platform music rights from rendering.
