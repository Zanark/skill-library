# Fully reviewed cinematic music video

Develop a continuous original full-song story with complete media/lyric-window review.

- **Inputs:** a permitted song/lyrics, original request time, story intent and
  permitted art/assets.
- **Mechanism:** new local story/compositor authoring and the explicit full-review
  procedure, not a sampled-only fast path.
- **Result:** a full-quality portrait film with lyric/progress, scene/join and audio evidence.
- **Review:** every lyric occurrence/reset/gap, scene/action/join and encoded clock.

Read [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) and [RUNBOOK.md](RUNBOOK.md). Use a current ClipFarm
checkout. Recognition remains estimated; unresolved conflicts block full
clearance. Direct listening, creative acceptance and upload are separate.
