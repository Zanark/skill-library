# Chaptered landscape teaching film

Carry complete narration through a padded room, internal board and persistent headings.

- **Inputs:** full permitted narration/art and one source-time chapter/action timeline.
- **Mechanism:** a freshly authored local producer; headings and description
  chapters derive from the same clock.
- **Result:** a full-length landscape teaching film with matching chapter timestamps.
- **Review:** complete audio, causal actions, joins, readable headings and final
  encoded chapter timing.

Read [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) and [RUNBOOK.md](RUNBOOK.md). Use a current ClipFarm
checkout. Do not import a native-solve intro hold or shorten the narration.
